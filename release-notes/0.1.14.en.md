- A song no longer cuts off halfway when the network or VPN server changes: the player gets a fresh address with pauses and, if the stream still fails, reopens the song where it was instead of skipping to the next one.
- Watches are no longer offered as a device to control music on.
- Search “All” now opens with the top result as a large card: an artist with Play and Open, an album or a song — no detour through the Artists tab.
