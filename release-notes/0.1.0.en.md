The first Melogold for Windows.

- Search YouTube Music and regular YouTube, Trends and New, albums, artists, channels and playlists.
- Library: Favorites, your playlists, History, saved albums and artists.
- Sync through the Melogold server: sign in, create an account, devices; edits from your phone arrive in seconds.
- Now playing with synced lyrics, lyrics search on LRCLIB.
- Queue, sleep timer, mini player, media keys and buttons on the taskbar thumbnail.
- Updates itself from GitHub.
