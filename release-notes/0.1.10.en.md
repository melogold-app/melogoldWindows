- Select several tracks the usual Windows way (Ctrl+click, Shift+click, Ctrl+A) to play them, queue them, add them to Favorites or a playlist, download them, or make a new playlist named after their common album.
- Lyrics you pick in “Find other lyrics” now show up on your other devices too.
- A track closed in your country says why: which country YouTube places you in and in how many countries the track is open.
- In a narrow window the volume stays: its button opens a slider.
- Lyrics editor: what you type on the Text tab shows up in Sync right away.
- The Now Playing background appears together with the content, not after it.
- After an update the taskbar icon changes to the new one.
