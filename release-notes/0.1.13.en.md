- Now Playing: after you edit the title, artist or album, the new text shows at once — on screen, in the queue and in the Windows media panel — no need to close and reopen.
- The big cover in Now Playing no longer leaves an empty spot: while it downloads, the small one from the player bar shows under it.
- Controlling another device: if the phone doesn't report the track length, it is taken from the track itself, so the time and slider are right instead of “0:08 / 0:00” with the slider at the end.
- Album and artist descriptions: “More” opens them in a separate window — title, year, cover, the full text and links to the Wikipedia article and its license. The double checkmark on the “In library” button is gone.
