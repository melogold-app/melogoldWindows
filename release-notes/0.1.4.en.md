- Listening history is shared between your devices: in History you can pick which device to show, and "Clear" and "Remove from history" work everywhere.
- Library import and export, like on Android: "Save a copy" and "Import a copy" in Settings, including copies from ViTune and Android.
- "All tracks" in the Library: everything you have played and saved, sorted by listening time, title, artist or duration.
- A 4 GB music cache: a track played to the end plays again without traffic or network; such tracks are marked and gathered in Downloads. The cache size is in Settings.
- One "…" menu in the player: the track actions as in lists, the lyrics, the sleep timer.
- Sign in on a watch and other Apple devices with a code: "Add a device" in your account.
- A rounded app icon, larger in the window's title bar.
